# SAARTHI Knowledge Base - Nepal Civic Technology Platform

## Civic & Government Services
- **MeroShare & NEPSE**: Demat verification, C-ASBA IPO applications, Meroshare login portal guidelines.
- **Driving License (DOTM)**: Online application (edivid.dotm.gov.np), biometric appointment, trial rules, license print status.
- **Vehicle Bluebook Tax**: Annual province-level vehicle tax rates based on CC for 2-wheelers and 4-wheelers.
- **Inland Revenue Department (IRD)**: Personal PAN card registration, e-TDS filing, Income Tax FY 2081/82 slabs and rates.
- **Lok Sewa Aayog**: Civil service examination syllabus, vacancy notices, admit card portal.
- **Passport & Citizenship**: e-Passport pre-enrollment system, NID (National Identity Card) biometric enrollment.
